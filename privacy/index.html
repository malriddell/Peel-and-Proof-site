Privacy policy

Effective January 2027

The short version: Peel & Proof doesn't collect any data about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app doesn't send what you do in it to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Settings, such as ounces or millilitres, and the ingredients you tick in "What can I make?", are stored only on your device.
  • Asking the bartender in "What can I make?" uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model. What you type is processed on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere, including to Apple's servers.
  • Scanning a bottle uses the camera to read the barcode and label on your iPhone. No photos are taken or kept, and nothing is sent anywhere. The camera is used only while you're scanning, and only if you allow it.

What syncs through your iCloud

The bottles on My Shelf, your favourite recipes and your build notes are saved in your own private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. We can't see it. You can turn this off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud, or delete it by deleting the app's iCloud data there.

Purchases

The Full Unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we receive no payment details and nothing that identifies you. The app checks with the App Store whether you own the unlock.

Network

The app connects only to Apple: the App Store for purchases and iCloud for syncing. It uses no third-party services, libraries or SDKs that send data.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from this site. Like any website, our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability; we don't use them to identify or track you.

Age

Peel & Proof is about alcoholic drinks and is meant only for adults of legal drinking age where they live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
