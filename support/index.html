Support

Something not working, a recipe that needs fixing, or an idea? Email dev-team@riddellapps.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something's broken.

Common questions

I bought the Full Unlock, but recipes are still locked.

Open Settings in the app (the gear on Library) and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Does my family get the unlock?

Yes. The Full Unlock works with Family Sharing. Family members get it automatically if they're in your Family Sharing group and have Purchase Sharing turned on; they may need to tap Restore Purchases.

My bottles don't show up on my other iPhone or iPad.

My Shelf, favourites, build notes and Masterclass tasting notes sync through iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, and that iCloud is on for Peel & Proof (Settings → [your name] → iCloud). Changes can take a minute or two to appear.

I don't see "Ask the bartender".

Asking the bartender needs the Full Unlock and an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on and finished downloading. Ticking what you have in "What can I make?" works on every iPhone, with or without it.

Where's the seasonal recipe?

Seasonal recipes are free for everyone during their season. Outside it, they're part of the Full Unlock.

Do you have a subscription?

No, and there won't be one. The Full Unlock is a one-time purchase.
