Drink responsibly

Peel & Proof is here to teach you how an Old Fashioned works and help you find one you enjoy. It isn't here to help you drink more.

How strong it is

An Old Fashioned is almost all spirit. A full drink in Peel & Proof is 2 oz of whiskey: about 1⅓ US standard drinks at 80 proof, and 1⅔ at 100 proof. A tasting pour is half that.

Every time

  • Only drink if you're of legal drinking age where you live.
  • Never drink and drive, or before anything that needs a clear head.
  • Eat something, and drink water alongside.
  • Know your limit and stop before it. You don't have to finish a drink to taste it.
  • Don't drink if you're pregnant or trying to be, if a medicine you take doesn't mix with alcohol, or if a doctor has told you not to.

In the Masterclass

Levels that compare drinks are split into sessions of one to three drinks. Do one session a day at most. Use tasting pours, share them, and pour out what's left once you've tasted it.

If drinking worries you

If you're worried about your drinking, or someone else's, talk to a doctor. In the US, the SAMHSA National Helpline is free, confidential and open every day, all day: 1-800-662-4357.

For adults of legal drinking age. Drink responsibly.
